Our Heritage

Redefining The Expedition

Born in the foothills of Mount Rinjani, we are a passionate team of local experts dedicated to elevating your wilderness adventure with unmatched comfort and safety.

The Grand Rinjani Journey

Where Raw Nature Meets
Premium Hospitality

Grand Rinjani was founded by a collective of senior guides from Senaru and Sembalun who shared a unified vision: to challenge the standard of Mount Rinjani trekking. For years, climbing this majestic volcano meant roughing it out. We asked, "Why can't we experience the raw, untamed beauty of the mountain while enjoying the comforts of a premium retreat?"

Today, we stand as the premier trekking operator in Lombok. By combining state-of-the-art camping gear, rigorous international safety protocols, and a team that prepares gourmet meals above the clouds, we have transformed the arduous trek into a luxurious expedition. Every step you take with us is guided by profound local knowledge and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our Pillars

What Sets Us Apart

We do not just guide you up a mountain; we craft an immersive, safe, and responsible journey.

Uncompromising Safety

Your well-being is our absolute priority. Our guides are certified in Wilderness First Aid, and our equipment is meticulously maintained to withstand harsh alpine conditions. We monitor weather patterns continuously and carry emergency communication devices.

Eco-Conscious Trekking

We operate a strict "Leave No Trace" policy. Our teams bring all waste down the mountain, utilize reusable containers where possible, and actively participate in seasonal mountain clean-up initiatives to preserve Rinjani for future generations.

Local Empowerment

Grand Rinjani is a proudly local enterprise. 100% of our guides, porters, and operational staff are from the surrounding villages. By choosing us, you directly contribute to fair wages and sustainable economic growth within the local community.

Culinary Excellence

Forget instant noodles. Our porters are trained mountain chefs who prepare fresh, highly nutritious, and delicious meals ranging from traditional Indonesian cuisine to Western favorites, ensuring you have the energy required for the demanding trek.
