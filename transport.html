Your Journey Begins Here

We ensure your expedition is hassle-free from the moment you arrive in Lombok. Enjoy comfortable, private, and air-conditioned transfers directly to your trekking basecamp.

First-Class Fleet & Professional Drivers

Navigating Lombok's scenic but winding mountain roads requires local expertise. Our drivers are highly experienced, ensuring a safe and smooth ride to Senaru, Sembalun, or Torean.

All of our multi-day trekking packages automatically **include complimentary pickup and drop-off** from major hubs in Lombok. However, if you are booking a custom trip or need standalone transport, our fleet is at your service.

  • Modern, Air-Conditioned Vehicles
  • Punctual & Reliable Pickups
  • Spacious Luggage Capacity

Our Pickup Coverage Zones

Wherever you are in Lombok, we will bring you directly to the mountain. Estimated travel times to Senaru/Sembalun basecamps are listed below.

Lombok Airport (ZAM)

Direct transfer upon your flight arrival.

2.5 - 3 Hours

Bangsal Harbor

Perfect if you are arriving from the Gili Islands or Bali.

1.5 - 2 Hours

Senggigi & Mataram

Pickups directly from your hotel lobby in the city or coastal area.

2 - 2.5 Hours

Kuta Lombok

Transfer from the beautiful southern surfing beaches.

3 - 3.5 Hours
